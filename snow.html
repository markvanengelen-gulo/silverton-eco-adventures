Snow

Winter brings spectacular white views, thrills and exercise in the Kootenays.

Valhalla and Kokanee Parks are the perfect spot for your snow adventures.

Activities

X-country Ski Tours

Join our guides on X-country ski tours for 1, 2 or 4 hours. Full day and multi day trips also available. Ski rental included, or bring your own!

Backcountry Ski Tours

We can help plan an unbelievable experience with expert backcountry guides, lodges and more!

Snowshoeing Tours

Join our guides on snowshoeing tours all across Slocan Valley for 1, 2 or 4 hours. Full day and multi day trips also available. Snowshoe rental included, or bring your own!

Downhill Ski or Snowboard Day

Join our guides for full day downhill ski or snowboard adventures at Summit, Whitewater, Red Mountain, Revelstoke or other great spots in the Kootenays.

X-country Ski & Hotsprings

Enjoy a nice X-country ski and conclude with some local hotsprings to relax.

Custom Multi Day Snow Trips

We’ll help plan a custom multi day trip in the Kootenays together with you!
