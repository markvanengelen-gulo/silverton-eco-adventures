Spotlight Adventure

Guided Shore Dive — Experienced SCUBA Divers

Guided dive in Slocan Lake, available year round! Explore underwater train cars, forests and find schools of trout and salmon.

Available upon request. Up to 1 hour of guided dive in Slocan or Kootenay Lake. Dive certificate required as proof, at least PADI Open Water or equivalent.
